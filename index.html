The fish that gets away teaches you more than the one you catch.

I once fought a marlin for four hours before the line broke, and I went home with nothing. The next weekend I went back and caught two.

That is the core of how I design. When something I make doesn't work, I take it as the most honest feedback I can get, and it sends me back to work with the same excitement I feel every time I leave the dock.

About

Go back out with a better approach.

Growing up on the water showed me that you cannot argue with the results, only learn from them, let them go, and go back out with a better approach.

My work at Gulf Marine has taught me that good design must go beyond appearance. Standing between the people who build boats and the people who buy them, I have seen that what a customer wants, what a builder can achieve, and what a designer envisions do not always align. I use sketching, CAD, and physical prototyping not only to develop ideas but to communicate them, test their feasibility, and find solutions that balance creativity with the realities of how something is built and used.
